# Verification

Run `./gradlew clean build contractTests`. The contract suite checks mesh triangulation and draw-batch scaling through 2,000 soldiers.

For an in-game smoke test:

Before visual testing on AMD, inspect `logs/recruits-performance-shaders`. Startup must log a successful minimal GLSL preflight followed by the crowd vertex and fragment source digests. On the affected RX 570, confirm initialization passes `glShaderSource`, shader compilation and program linking without creating a new `hs_err_pid*.log`.

1. Install Recruits and this mod, start a Forge 1.20.1 client, and create a disposable world.
2. Spawn human and villager recruits with no equipment. Confirm they render once and remain depth-occluded by terrain.
3. Equip distinct vanilla and all supplied Epic Knights addon armor/decorations in every slot on both human and villager recruits, plus swords, polearms, bows, crossbows and shields. Compare `magistuarmory:gambeson_chestplate` directly between a player, armor stand and recruit; confirm its dyed cloth comes from `gambeson_layer_1.png` while straps and buckles come from `gambeson_layer_1_overlay.png`. Test chest, leg and head pieces from 7 Years War Uniforms 1.4.2, Victorian Uniforms 1.0.0 and SYW USA 1.0.0 on multiple recruits; confirm their custom body, sleeve, trouser and hat geometry is complete and the equipment mesh count stabilizes after the first frame. Confirm each component follows the correct animated limb; verify two-sided plates, every dyeable base/overlay pair and patterned surcoats.
4. Change equipment while recruits are visible. Confirm only the changed cached component and batch counters change.
5. Exercise idle, walk, run, attack, eat, drink, block, bow, crossbow, hurt and death states, plus Recruits RTS formation movement. With the supplied Better Combat, Better Mob Combat, Mob Player Animator and Recruits Serious Animations 0.13.7 builds installed, confirm SPA eating and other non-combat specialty actions remain active, while Better Combat's real holding, swing, body and held-item channels become authoritative only during its active attacks without fallback swings, doubled poses, twitching or quantized snapping.
6. Reload resources with F3+T and repeat the checks. GPU objects must be rebuilt without duplicate soldiers or stale textures.
7. Verify human and villager biome textures, faction cloth and companion/assassin cloth match vanilla rendering from every camera angle; give one recruit a non-armor head item and confirm it deliberately stays on the vanilla fallback path.
8. Mount recruits on adult and foal Icy's Better Horses 2.1.6 breeds. Verify coat, saddle, carpet, armor, neck plates and chest tack stay synchronized; verify riders follow Icy's native saddle translation, pitch, roll and Serious Animations seat anchor while the horse moves. Equipped stabilizers/banners must use the original renderer.
9. With Cruor 0.1.3 hotfix installed, blood a recruit's held weapon. Confirm its separate stain pass never displays unrelated atlas sprites and is omitted beyond the configured stain distance while the base weapon remains instanced.
10. Damage unarmored human and villager recruits with blunt, sharp and piercing hits inside the configured Cruor detail distance. Confirm Cruor wounds appear on the correct animated body surface, remain aligned during native locomotion and Better Combat poses, respect Recruits team/biome cloth masks, dry normally, and remain depth-occluded by armor. Repeat with Oculus enabled.
11. Verify a nameplate appears only while the crosshair is over a Recruits unit; point at its horse and unrelated living entities and confirm no GPU nameplate is queued. Also verify ground shadows, enchanted glint fallback, glowing outlines and source death presentation.
12. Order recruits across open ground and around obstacles. Confirm native pathfinding and native movement speed remain unchanged with Recruits Performance installed.
13. Test with Oculus 1.8.0 both without and with the target shader pack, including daylight, fog, rain, shadowed scenes and the HUD hidden with F1. Confirm recruits remain visible in the correct world position after Oculus' final composite, no vanilla armor layer reappears, and instanced draw calls remain active in both modes; compare frame time at equal recruit counts with an external profiler.
14. Compare recruits with a vanilla living entity at noon, midnight, underground at block light 0, beside a torch, during rain and in the Nether. Confirm bodies, armor, held items and horses darken and tint with Minecraft's live lightmap instead of remaining full-bright; repeat with non-default gamma and Oculus enabled.
15. Join a dedicated server that does not have Recruits Performance installed and confirm the client connects normally; native server AI, orders, pathfinding and movement must remain unchanged.
16. Set `enabled=false`; restart and confirm the original Recruits renderer is restored for A/B comparison.
17. Equip `betsyross:flag_standard` and `betsyross:banner_standard` with several built-in and uploaded Immersive Paintings selections. Confirm the pole uses its normal atlas material, both sides use only the selected painting, texture changes invalidate the correct cached pass, and no Minecraft atlas sheet appears on the cloth.

Run the scenarios in [BENCHMARKING.md](BENCHMARKING.md) on the target RX 570. The cloud build environment can compile and validate CPU contracts but cannot verify real OpenGL driver behavior, visual equivalence, shader-pack interoperability or GPU frame time.
